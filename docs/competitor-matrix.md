# KOHLER AI BathPlan — Competitor Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix is designed to prevent weak competition claims and to identify exactly where BathPlan must be differentiated.

### Evidence legend

- **✓** = publicly documented capability
- **~** = adjacent/partial capability or marketing claim requiring qualification
- **?** = not established from the reviewed public evidence
- **—** = not a meaningful focus of the product

> A “?” does not mean the competitor cannot do it; it means we do not have sufficient public evidence to claim it.

### Critical reading

The table intentionally avoids giving competitors a “no” where public evidence is incomplete.

The most important confirmed competitive fact is:

> Planner 5D publicly markets AI bathroom planning that considers dimensions, fixture sizes, flow and plumbing constraints.

Therefore BathPlan cannot claim “we are the only AI bathroom planner that respects constraints.”

The stronger claim is:

> **BathPlan makes constraint validation explicit, inspectable and central to a KOHLER-specific recommendation-and-repair loop.**

# Competitor notes

## Planner 5D

Sources:
- https://planner5d.com/use/ai-bathroom-design
- https://planner5d.com/use/bathroom-planner-tool

### Strength
Full planning/visualization workflow.

### Competitive threat
It already addresses dimensions, fixture placement and plumbing-related planning.

### BathPlan response
Do not compete on generic planning. Compete on:
- KOHLER-specific structured product data
- deterministic rule engine
- product-level evidence
- explicit infeasibility
- quantified trade-offs
- conversational re-optimization

---

## Homestyler

Sources:
- https://www.homestyler.com/
- https://www.homestyler.com/about/updates

### Strength
Broad AI-assisted design and increasingly conversational/agentic workflows.

### Competitive threat
“AI agent for interior design” is not unique.

### BathPlan response
Make the agent's output a **validated configuration**, not primarily an image.

---

## Houzz

Source:
- https://www.houzz.com/

### Strength
Large inspiration/product/professional ecosystem.

### Competitive threat
Product discovery + design + professionals already coexist.

### BathPlan response
Own the moment before professional engagement: resolve configuration uncertainty.

---

## RoomGPT-style tools

Representative:
- https://www.roomgpt.io/

### Strength
Fast visual transformation.

### Weakness for this use case
Visual plausibility does not establish physical feasibility, product compatibility or budget.

### BathPlan response
Use image input only as an input modality. Do not make generated imagery the proof of correctness.

---

## Bathroom-specific AI tools

Examples:
- https://home-design.ai/ai-bathroom-design
- https://www.myarchitectai.com/bathroom-design-generator

### Strategic lesson
The category already exists.

### BathPlan response
The differentiated output is a **decision package**, not just a concept image.

# Judge-safe competitive statement

Avoid:

> “Existing AI bathroom tools don't understand constraints.”

Use:

> “Existing planners already provide increasingly sophisticated layout and constraint features. BathPlan focuses on making the decision loop explicit: real KOHLER product facts → candidate configuration → deterministic validation → quantified trade-offs → conversational repair.”

That statement is narrower and easier to defend.
